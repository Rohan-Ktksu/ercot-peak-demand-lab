01 / THE MACHINE LEARNING PIPELINERECORDED RESEARCH OUTPUTS
FROM DATA TO A PEAK-DEMAND WINDOW

Every step. Every output.

The experiment has already been completed in Python. Follow its recorded outputs below, then enter a date. No browser training or dataset upload is needed.

  1. 01

    Import and profile the datasets

    EIA-930 ERCOT reported demand and archived JMA weather forecasts for four Texas cities.

    Loading recorded dataset summary…
  2. 02

    Clean and validate

    Parse UTC timestamps, convert hour-ending demand to interval starts, check numeric ranges and duplicate keys, and require complete local-day targets. Missing actual demand is never invented.

  3. 03

    Extract and engineer features

    Calendar and cyclic time encodings; 1/2/7/14-day demand shapes, lagged demand and previous peak timing; archived forecast temperature, humidity and wind. Only inputs available under the documented issue-time assumptions are used.

  4. 04

    Split chronologically

    Five expanding time-series folds, each with 365 validation days and a two-day gap. Split whole days; never shuffle future observations into training.

  5. 05

    Fit preprocessing and select features

    Fit imputers and scaling inside training folds where a model needs them. Boosted trees handle missing inputs natively. HGB permutation importance and correlation pruning compare budgets of 12, 24 and 40 inputs.

    Inspect the selected input names
  6. 06

    Train, cross-validate and compare

    Compare baselines, Logistic Regression, Random Forest, Extra Trees, histogram boosting, XGBoost classification/ranking and regression variants. These are saved five-fold outputs, not a new training run.

    Selection rule: choose the smallest compact configuration within one percentage point of the best compact CV coverage, then break ties by coverage and exact-hour accuracy.

  7. 07

    Refit the selected model and inspect diagnostics

    2024–2025 were previously inspected and are reused diagnostics. These scores do not establish future accuracy; model scores are not calibrated probabilities. Compare all models and limitations ↓

  8. 08

    Enter a date and predict

    2024–2025: run the fitted XGBoost model on archived inputs. 2026–2030: estimate a three-hour window from historical month/weekday patterns, without synthetic data. The optional synthetic-input scenario remains available separately.

    Choose a date below ↓

Scope & limitations
  • This predicts demand timing, not outages, causes or restoration.
  • Demand is ERCOT-reported served load, not every part of Texas or an independently metered county.
  • 2024–2025 are reused historical diagnostics. 2026 is not evaluated.
  • The two-hour load delay and six-hour weather allowance are research assumptions, not verified feed guarantees.
  • Four city points are weather proxies; historical demand may be revised.
  • December 4–5, 2025 have incomplete actual demand and are excluded.
  • 70% exact-hour accuracy has not been achieved.
